Direction B — Grouped accordion. The 47 instances fall into six families that already exist in their names: Featured, Most read, Latest, Topic, World Cup, Other. Each family is a section you can collapse. The toolbar sticks to the top of the screen, so search and filters stay reachable however far down you are.

Why this one: it matches how the shelves are actually maintained. "Update all the Most read shelves" is one section, not eleven scattered cards. The grouping is derived from the names you already use, so there is nothing new to keep in sync.
