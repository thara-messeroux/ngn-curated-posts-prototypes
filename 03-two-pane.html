Direction C — Two-pane workbench. A searchable index on the left that never moves, and the selected shelf's settings on the right. Picking a different shelf swaps the right pane only, so your place in the list is never lost — the thing that makes editing six shelves in a row painful today.

Why this one: it is built for the real task, which is rarely "look at one shelf". It is "work through the Latest shelves". The left pane is the whole screen's worth of context in 340px, and the right pane has room to show the slots a shelf actually fills.
